SAMJHAO Four useful grammar friendsGrammar ke chaar dost

Ism — Noun

A name of a person, place, animal, or thing.Kisi shakhs, jagah, janwar ya cheez ka naam.

Fail — Verb

An action or work.Koi kaam ya harkat.

Sifat — Adjective

A word that tells an ism's quality.Jo lafz ism ki khoobi ya khasiyat bataye.

Jumlah — Sentence

Words joined to give a complete thought.Aise alfaaz ka majmua jo poora khayal de.
